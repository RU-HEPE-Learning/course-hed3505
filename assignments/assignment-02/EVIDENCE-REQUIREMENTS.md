# Evidence Requirements — Assignment 02

Required evidence:

1. selected indicator from WS1;
2. construct / characteristic to be measured;
3. proposed instrument or data-collection method;
4. example item / observation / recording rule;
5. proposed scoring or recording approach;
6. expected evidence produced by the instrument;
7. at least one instrument-design risk such as ambiguity, bias, mismatch, or unclear scoring;
8. rationale for the selected instrument.

Do not invent school results or participant responses.
