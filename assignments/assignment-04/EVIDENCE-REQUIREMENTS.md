# Evidence Requirements — Assignment 04

Required evidence:

1. selected approved data/evidence;
2. finding stated directly from the data;
3. interpretation;
4. at least one alternative explanation;
5. contradictory or missing evidence;
6. additional evidence needed;
7. confidence level;
8. justified decision or recommendation;
9. one claim that should **not** be made from the current evidence.

Use only approved baseline data. Unsupported causal claims are not permitted.
