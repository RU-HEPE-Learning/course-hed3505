## Use evidence

Consider:

- Item ↔ Objective / Indicator alignment
- Content validity
- IOC evidence
- Reliability evidence where appropriate
- Limitations of the available evidence

## Important data rule

IOC and reliability datasets used for practice must be labeled **INSTRUCTIONAL / SIMULATED DATA** unless they are documented as real data.

Do not treat simulated expert ratings or response data as evidence from โรงเรียนอรุณพัฒนา.
