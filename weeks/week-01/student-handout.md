# Student Handout — Module 1

## Challenge

Choose one approved issue from the โรงเรียนอรุณพัฒนา teaching case and convert it into an indicator that can be evaluated.

## Use only approved evidence

Separate:

- FACT
- INTERPRETATION
- ASSUMPTION
- UNKNOWN

## Decide

You must decide:
- which indicator best represents the issue;
- why it is appropriate;
- what additional evidence is still needed.

**WS1 Assessment Planning**
