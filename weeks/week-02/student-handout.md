# Student Handout — Module 2

## Challenge

Use the indicator from WS1 to design an instrument blueprint.

## Decide

You must decide:
- what construct should be measured;
- which instrument or method fits the indicator;
- what item / observation / scoring method should be used;
- what design risks may weaken the evidence.

Do not create school results in advance. This module designs how evidence will be collected; it does not fabricate the evidence.
