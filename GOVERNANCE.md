# Governance

## Operating principles
Evidence-First / No Fabrication / Human Academic Authority Preserved.

Claims should be classified as FACT, INTERPRETATION, RECOMMENDATION, or UNKNOWN. Conflicts are marked EVIDENCE CONFLICT / REVIEW REQUIRED.

## Human authority boundaries
Instructor approval is required for official scores, grades, CLO/PLO mapping, assessment weights, deadlines, roster binding, privacy exceptions, and public release of real student work.

## Data policy
This pilot uses instructional/synthetic case data only. Do not store real student health data, national IDs, phone numbers, home addresses, or other sensitive personal data in public repositories.
