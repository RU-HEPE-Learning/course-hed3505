# Rubric Master Framework

Each assignment rubric uses criteria with: Evidence expected, performance descriptors, instructor judgment, and feedback space.

Common descriptors:
- **Strong evidence use** — claims are traceable, distinctions are accurate, limitations are explicit.
- **Developing** — generally relevant but some links, distinctions, or limitations need clarification.
- **Insufficient evidence** — major claims are unsupported, required evidence is missing, or interpretation exceeds available evidence.

SCORE / WEIGHT: PENDING COURSE AUTHORITY.

Automated checks must not convert descriptors into official grades.
